Frequently asked questions

Do I need any coding background to attend?

No. The conclave is built for first-time coders — every activity starts from block-based coding before touching any code.

Can I attend on behalf of my school without a principal?

Yes — teachers, coordinators and ICT in-charges are all welcome. We simply ask that a decision-maker join the Day 3 partnership track if possible.

Is there a cost to attend?

Registration details and any applicable fees will be shared once you reserve your seat below.

What do I need to bring?

Just a laptop. PictoBlox runs without any special hardware for the core sessions.
